HOW WE WORK

Stages and
timelines

1. Audit and plan · 1–7 days

We map how a lead travels through your business today and where it gets stuck (1–2 days), then show a prototype of the new flow and the system layout (3–5 days).

2. Build and connect

Website, bot, CRM and automations are built and connected on a test environment. The timeline depends on how many parts and integrations you need.

3. Test, launch, support

We run real requests through the whole chain (2–4 days), launch (1 day) and train your team. Then we stay on support so the system keeps working as you grow.
