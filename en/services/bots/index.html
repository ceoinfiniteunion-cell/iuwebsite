THE PROBLEM

Where a bot
saves you time

Your team answers the same ten questions every day: prices, opening hours, availability, delivery. Orders arrive as private messages, some get lost, and anything that comes in after 6 pm waits until the morning.

A Telegram bot takes over that routine. It shows the menu or catalogue, collects the order or booking, takes payment and sends a complete request to your team, so a manager only steps in where a person is really needed.

We build bots as proper applications, with a database, an admin panel and error handling, not as a script that stops working when Telegram changes something. You get the full source code and the bot account.

WHAT'S INCLUDED

What goes into
a business bot

01

Conversation flows

We map every step of the dialogue: buttons, questions, branches and what happens if the user leaves halfway. Flows run on a finite state machine with Redis, so conversations do not get stuck.
FSMRedisaiogram 3
02

Database and CRM sync

Every order or lead is saved to PostgreSQL and sent to your manager. We can also push data to Google Sheets, Notion or your CRM through its API.
PostgreSQLGoogle SheetsREST API
03

Payments in the chat

Customers pay without leaving Telegram. We connect Stripe, Telegram Payments, LiqPay, WayForPay or Monobank, with automatic confirmation and receipts.
StripeTelegram PaymentsLiqPay
04

Admin panel and hosting

You manage content, orders and broadcasts from an admin menu inside Telegram. The bot is deployed on Railway or your own server, with automatic restarts and error logs.
